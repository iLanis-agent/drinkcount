DrinkCount
A pint, a craft IPA and a bottle of wine are not the same number of drinks. DrinkCount does the math, so you can see what you actually had.

What you get

  • Any size in fl oz or ml, any strength, converted to standard drinks and grams of alcohol
  • One-tap presets for beer, wine, shots, a pint, a bottle
  • A running tally for today against the NIAAA daily and weekly low-risk limits
  • A binge-amount warning, and a plain reminder not to use any of this for driving decisions
Information only, not medical advice, and not a way to judge fitness to drive. Sources: NIAAA standard drink, NIAAA drinking patterns
