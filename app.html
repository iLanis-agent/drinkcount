About this

One US standard drink is about 14 g (0.6 fl oz) of pure alcohol: 12 fl oz of 5% beer, 5 fl oz of 12% wine, 1.5 fl oz of 40% spirits. Other countries define a unit differently. The limits are NIAAA low-risk limits, which are not a safe level and do not mean it is safe to drive. People who are pregnant, on medication, or with health conditions should drink less or not at all. Not medical advice.
